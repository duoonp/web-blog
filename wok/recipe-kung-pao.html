Kung-Pao Chicken

A classic Sichuan dish featuring an addictive combination of spicy, sweet, and savory flavors with a satisfying crunch from roasted peanuts.

Ingredients:

  • Chicken: 400g chicken breast or thigh, diced into cubes
  • Marinade: 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp Shaoxing wine, 1 tsp cornstarch
  • Aromatics: 3 cloves garlic (minced), 1 tbsp ginger (minced), 5-8 dried red chilies, 1 tsp Sichuan peppercorns
  • Vegetables & Nuts: 1 bell pepper (diced), 3 scallions (chopped), 1/2 cup roasted unsalted peanuts
  • Sauce: 2 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tbsp dark vinegar (Chinkiang), 1 tbsp sugar, 1 tsp sesame oil, 1 tsp cornstarch mixed with 2 tbsp water

Instructions:

  1. Marinate the chicken: Toss the diced chicken with soy sauce, Shaoxing wine, and cornstarch. Let it sit for 15-20 minutes.
  2. Prepare the sauce: In a small bowl, whisk together the soy sauce, vinegar, sugar, sesame oil, and cornstarch slurry.
  3. Fry the aromatics: Heat 2 tablespoons of oil in your wok over high heat. Add the dried chilies and Sichuan peppercorns, stirring for 30 seconds until fragrant (don't burn them).
  4. Cook the chicken: Add the marinated chicken to the wok. Stir-fry until browned and mostly cooked through (about 4-5 minutes).
  5. Add veggies and sauce: Toss in the garlic, ginger, scallions, and bell peppers. Stir-fry for 1 minute. Pour in the prepared sauce.
  6. Finish: Toss everything continuously as the sauce thickens and coats the chicken. Stir in the roasted peanuts right at the end. Serve immediately with rice!
