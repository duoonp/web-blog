Kung-Pao Chicken

Crispy, garlicky, and perfectly seasoned. Leaving the shells on protects the meat from overcooking and holds maximum flavor.

Ingredients:

  • Shrimps: 400g large raw shrimps (preferably shell-on, deveined, with legs trimmed)
  • Coating: 1/3 cup cornstarch or potato starch
  • The Spice Mix: 1 tsp sea salt, 1 tsp freshly ground black pepper, 1/2 tsp white pepper, 1/2 tsp toasted Sichuan peppercorn powder, 1/4 tsp sugar
  • Aromatics: 1 jalapeño or red chili (thinly sliced), 4 cloves garlic (finely minced), 3 scallions (chopped)
  • Oil: Neutral oil for shallow frying (about 1/2 cup)

Instructions:

  1. Prep the spice mix: In a small bowl, combine the salt, black pepper, white pepper, Sichuan pepper, and sugar. Set aside.
  2. Prep the shrimps: Rinse the shrimps and pat them completely dry with paper towels (important for crispiness). Toss them in the cornstarch until evenly coated. Shake off the excess.
  3. Fry the shrimps: Heat the oil in your wok over medium-high heat. Fry the shrimps in two batches for about 1.5 to 2 minutes per side until pink and crispy. Remove and drain on a wire rack or paper towels.
  4. Aromatics: Drain all but 1 tablespoon of oil from the wok. Turn heat to medium. Add the minced garlic, sliced chili, and scallions. Stir-fry for 30 seconds until very fragrant.
  5. Toss and season: Add the crispy shrimps back into the wok. Sprinkle half of the spice mix over the top. Toss vigorously for 20 seconds so the shrimps are coated in the aromatics and spices.
  6. Taste and serve: Taste one and add more spice mix if needed. Serve hot immediately!
