Kung-Pao Chicken

Silky tofu in a fiery, numbing, and deeply savory sauce. This is the ultimate comfort food for spice lovers.

Ingredients:

  • Tofu: 1 block (400g) soft or silken tofu, cut into 1-inch cubes
  • Meat: 150g ground pork or beef
  • Base flavor: 2 tbsp Doubanjiang (spicy broad bean paste), 1 tbsp fermented black beans (douchi), rinsed and chopped
  • Aromatics: 1 tbsp minced garlic, 1 tbsp minced ginger, 2 scallions (whites and greens separated)
  • Spices: 1 tbsp ground Sichuan peppercorns (toasted), 1 tsp chili oil (optional)
  • Liquids: 1 cup chicken broth, 1 tbsp soy sauce, 1 tsp sugar
  • Thickener: 1.5 tbsp cornstarch mixed with 3 tbsp cold water

Instructions:

  1. Blanch the tofu: Carefully simmer the tofu cubes in salted boiling water for 2 minutes to help them hold their shape. Drain gently.
  2. Brown the meat: Heat oil in the wok over medium-high heat. Add the ground pork/beef and stir-fry until crispy and brown.
  3. Fry the pastes: Turn the heat to medium. Add the Doubanjiang and fermented black beans to the oil. Fry until the oil turns red and fragrant (about 1-2 minutes).
  4. Aromatics: Add garlic, ginger, and the white parts of the scallions. Stir for 30 seconds.
  5. Simmer: Pour in the chicken broth, soy sauce, and sugar. Bring to a simmer, then gently slide in the tofu cubes. Let it bubble gently for 3-5 minutes.
  6. Thicken: Slowly stir in the cornstarch slurry in three batches, gently pushing the tofu with the back of your spatula so it doesn't break. The sauce should become thick and glossy.
  7. Garnish: Turn off the heat, stir in the chili oil. Top generously with the ground Sichuan peppercorns and the green parts of the scallions.
